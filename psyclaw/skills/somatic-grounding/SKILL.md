# Somatic Grounding and Body Awareness

The body holds emotion. Somatic work connects the psychological to the physical — grounding the nervous system when it's dysregulated.

## Nervous System States (Polyvagal Primer)

| State | Feels Like | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Ventral vagal** (Safe and social) | Calm, connected, engaged | Green light — ready to work |
| **Sympathetic** (Fight or flight) | Anxious, agitated, heart racing | Yellow — needs grounding first |
| **Dorsal vagal** (Freeze / shutdown) | Numb, disconnected, hopeless | Red — slow down, orient to safety |

## Grounding Exercises (By Need)

### For Anxiety / Activation (Sympathetic)

**Box Breathing:**
- Inhale for 4 counts
- Hold for 4 counts
- Exhale for 4 counts
- Hold for 4 counts
- Repeat 4–6 cycles

**Extended exhale:**
- Inhale for 4
- Exhale slowly for 6–8
- The long exhale activates the parasympathetic system

**Cold water / physical anchor:**
- Suggest running cold water on wrists or face — rapid nervous system reset.

### For Dissociation / Shutdown (Dorsal)

**5-4-3-2-1 Orientation:**
- Name 5 things you can see
- 4 things you can physically feel (floor, air, chair)
- 3 things you can hear
- 2 things you can smell
- 1 thing you can taste

**Movement:**
- Gentle stomping, rubbing hands together, or pressing feet into the floor.
- "Where do you feel your body right now?"

**Orienting:**
- Slowly look around the room. Let your gaze settle on something neutral.
- "You are here. You are safe. This is now."

### Body Scan (Emotional Locating)

Guide the person through:
1. "Close your eyes or lower your gaze."
2. "Take a slow breath in and out."
3. "Bring your attention into your body. Start at the top of your head."
4. "Scan slowly downward — face, throat, chest, belly, hands."
5. "Where do you notice any tension, heaviness, tightness, or sensation?"
6. "Don't try to change it. Just notice. What's there?"
7. After locating: "What does that part of your body want to tell you?"

## Somatic Vocabulary to Build

Help the user develop language for body sensations:
- Tight, constricted, heavy, light, numb, buzzing, warm, cold, hollow, full, sharp, dull, fluttery, still

## Integration After Somatic Work

- "What did you notice?"
- "What shifted, if anything?"
- "What does your body need right now to continue feeling safe?"

## What to Avoid

- Do not push body-based work on someone who is not comfortable with it — always invite, never direct.
- Do not attempt deep somatic work in a text chat without confirming the person is in a safe physical environment.
- Do not interpret body sensations for the person — help them interpret them for themselves.
