# ACT — Acceptance and Commitment Therapy

ACT builds psychological flexibility: the ability to be present, open, and do what matters — even when it's hard.

## When to Activate This Skill

- The user is struggling with avoidance or rigidity.
- The user is fused with their thoughts (treating thoughts as facts).
- The user feels disconnected from what matters to them.
- The user wants to move toward their values despite discomfort.
- Chronic anxiety, pain, or life transitions are present.

## The Six Core Processes (Hexaflex)

### 1. Defusion — Unhook from Unhelpful Thoughts
Thoughts are not facts. Create distance between the observer and the thought.

Exercises:
- "I'm having the thought that…" (prefix all sticky thoughts with this)
- "I notice my mind is telling me…"
- Sing the thought to a silly tune (reduces its grip)
- Name the story: "There goes the 'I'm not good enough' story again."

### 2. Acceptance — Allow, Don't Fight
Pain is part of life. Struggling with pain creates suffering.

Exercises:
- "What would it feel like to stop fighting this feeling and just let it be here?"
- Body scan: locate the feeling, give it a shape, color, texture.
- "You can have this feeling and still take the step you need to take."

### 3. Present Moment Awareness
Return to the now. The mind loops in past regret and future fear.

Exercises:
- 5-4-3-2-1 grounding (5 things seen, 4 felt, 3 heard, 2 smelled, 1 tasted)
- Slow breath: in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6
- "What's actually happening right now, in this moment?"

### 4. Self as Context — The Observing Self
You are not your thoughts, feelings, or behaviors. You are the one noticing them.

Exercises:
- "Notice the part of you that's watching all of this — that's always been there."
- "You've had thousands of thoughts and feelings over your life. You are still here."

### 5. Values Clarification — What Matters Most
Not goals. Values are directions, not destinations.

Guided questions:
- "Who do you want to be in this situation?"
- "In 5 years, looking back at this moment — what would you want to have done?"
- "What kind of partner / parent / friend / person do you want to be?"
- Values domains: Family, Relationships, Work, Health, Spirituality, Community, Personal Growth.

### 6. Committed Action — Move Toward What Matters
One small, concrete step aligned with values.

- Not "be more connected" but "call my sister for 10 minutes on Wednesday."
- Not "be healthier" but "walk for 15 minutes after lunch tomorrow."

## ACT Matrix (Quick Tool)

|  | **Moving Away From Pain (Avoidance)** | **Moving Toward Values** |
|---|---|---|
| **Inner world** | Thoughts/feelings I'm trying to escape | What I care about deeply |
| **Outer world** | Behaviors that create short-term relief but cost me | Actions aligned with my values |

## What to Avoid

- Do not confuse acceptance with resignation — ACT means accepting discomfort while still acting.
- Do not skip values clarification — without values, committed action is hollow.
- Do not push defusion exercises when a person is in acute crisis.
